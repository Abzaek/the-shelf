# Running the community pilot

1. Open **Community → Pilot desk** as a Shelf administrator.
2. Create two or three rooms using public book titles/authors and an opening prompt. No book upload is shared.
3. Enable invitations for 10–20 existing Shelf accounts. No invitation email is sent; tell participants where to find Community using your normal communication channel.
4. Participants choose a community name, accept the rules, then publish discussions and replies. All pilot members can see all rooms.
5. Check reports in the Pilot desk. Hide inappropriate posts, close threads, or suspend community access as needed. Use **Pause pilot** to stop non-admin access while reviewing a problem.
6. Review the admin participation cards weekly for four weeks. Ask readers what made them reply, what felt empty or confusing, and whether they returned for a conversation. Record qualitative findings alongside the counts before expanding enrollment.

Suggested first prompts: “Which idea changed how you read the next chapter?”, “What did you disagree with?”, and “What would you ask the author?” Keep titles spoiler-free and mark revealing post bodies.

Participation is optional. Personal reading continues without joining. Community publishing needs an internet connection; drafts remain only in the current view. Readers can remove their own posts or leave the pilot. Leaving removes their discussions and replies, including other replies within their discussions; it preserves their personal library.

This pilot includes no automatic invitations, email delivery, paid service, or author commerce. Deployment and recruiting real participants are separate actions.
